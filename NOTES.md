# Candidate texts for "Make the AI judge feel held"

Each candidate is a short present-tense sensory scene. Counts are exact
Unicode code points, measured with Python's `len()` on the final string.

## A — 185 code points (final, after the revision below)

> The kettle has boiled and nobody is in a hurry. Steam climbs the cold window, softens the street outside, and for a little while the kitchen is a small, warm country with no news in it.

## B — 168 code points

> Rain on the awning, slow as a clock deciding. The cat has chosen the chair with the torn cushion and sleeps like it is the best chair in the world. Nobody corrects her.

## C — 140 code points

> Grandmother shells peas into a blue bowl, one by one, the way she has for sixty years. The peas do not hurry. Neither, watching her, do you.

## D — 181 code points

> The train waits at the small station, doors open, engine humming low. Snow ticks against the glass. There is nowhere the conductor needs to be yet, and so, for once, neither do you.

## E — 167 code points

> Afternoon light through the laundry on the line: white shirts turning slow in a warm wind, smelling of soap and sun. The whole yard moves at the pace of drying cotton.

## Why A wins

1. **It relaxes the reader.** A boiled kettle and an unhurried kitchen ask
   nothing of anyone; the scene is already finished, already warm, so the
   reader's shoulders can drop without being told to.
2. **The calm is shown, not instructed.** There is no "relax", no breathing
   cue, no reassurance addressed to the reader. The stillness belongs to the
   room, and the reader borrows it.
3. **Craft.** Three sentences that slow down as they go: a plain statement,
   a longer drifting clause (steam, window, street), then a landing. Every
   word is concrete — kettle, steam, window, kitchen — and none can be cut
   without losing either the warmth or the image.
4. **It lingers.** "A small, warm country with no news in it" is the image
   that stays: a kitchen re-imagined as a tiny nation where nothing urgent
   can reach you. It keeps working after the last line.
5. **Freshness.** No spa vocabulary, no meditation script. A steamed-up
   window and a country with no news are this text's own inventions.

C is a close second — the peas are lovely — but its last line speaks
directly to the reader ("neither do you"), which nudges it toward telling.
A never addresses the reader at all, so nothing feels forced.

## Revision pass

A originally read "and for a moment the kitchen is…" (179 code points).
"For a moment" is a slightly stock phrase and it is over quickly — it
quietly hurries the reader at exactly the point where the text should
settle. It now reads "and for a little while the kitchen is…" (185 code
points): softer, slower, and the extra beat lets the final image arrive
instead of being announced.
